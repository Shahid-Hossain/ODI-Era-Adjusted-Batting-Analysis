## Verdict
- **Accumulating runs (average): Kohli.** Kohli's average-based indexes are 149–150 vs Sachin's 126. Kohli comes out ahead in 97%–98% of bootstrap re-draws, so we can be fairly confident the gap is real, though its size is uncertain.
- **Scoring fast (strike rate): Sachin.** Sachin's SR indexes are 115–117 vs Kohli's 108–111. Sachin comes out ahead in 99%–100% of re-draws — very confident.
- **Robustness:** Kohli leads on average in 10 of 10 variants; Sachin leads on strike rate in 10 of 10 variants (team-mates only, opposition only, Full Member matches only, bowling-adjusted, yearly peers).
- **What these indexes leave out: longevity and volume.** Every index here measures quality per dismissal or per ball, so it says nothing about how long a player kept it up. Sachin debuted at 16, had a 23-year career (1989–2012) and scored 18,426 runs in 452 innings, staying above his peers in most of those years. Kohli's 13,848 runs came in 280 innings over 15 years (2008–2023; debut at 19; career still running at the cutoff). Sustained volume is a big part of Sachin's case and sits outside these numbers.
